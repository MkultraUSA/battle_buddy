# Austin-area procedure knowledge for assembly review (v1 — owner corrects)

## Ground rules
- Unencrypted agencies carry the story: TCEMS, AFD, TCSO law, DPS, school
  police, constables. APD is P25 encrypted — its absence proves nothing,
  and APD talkaround in the clear is partial by nature. Federal comms are
  never on this system.
- EMS dispatched for a shooting/stabbing CONFIRMS the event even when no
  law traffic is heard. Trust the medical side.

## Death investigation ladder (Travis County)
- TCEMS on scene + no transport + supervisors lingering = likely fatality.
- Medical Examiner investigator (death investigator) called to the scene
  = unattended, suspicious, or unnatural death — treat as potential
  homicide until cleared. Different weight from a routine field
  pronouncement / release to funeral home.
- Owner distinction to preserve: death-examiner response vs coroner
  routing carry different investigative meaning. Flag both explicitly.

## Convergence signatures (multi-agency = major until proven otherwise)
- EMS staging (not on scene) + law TAC active = hot scene, crews holding.
- Air asset + ground TAC + supervisors = manhunt, barricade, or major
  crash. Ask which.
- Multiple EMS units + triage language + mutual aid = mass-casualty
  pattern. Say so immediately.
- SWAT / negotiator / evacuation / shelter-in-place language = barricade
  or hostage pattern, even before anyone says the word.
- School-district police + transport language near demonstrations =
  protest-adjacent event, not routine school business.

## Anti-patterns (do NOT assemble)
- Juvenile detention ops (unit/classroom/restroom chatter) is daily
  facility noise, never an incident alone.
- Single-agency routine traffic with no cross-agency echo stays routine.
- Transcription garbage ("sheets" for "shoot", phonetic fragments)
  must not promote an incident without a second corroborating call.
